Common questions

How do I count a row? Tap the big +. Tapped by mistake? Tap Undo, or tap ↺ next to any History entry to go back to that count.

Repeat counters. For “repeat rows 1–8, 39 times”, add an extra counter, keep it linked, set “repeat every” to 8 and the repeat goal to 39.

Moving to a new iPhone. In Rowbin, go to Settings → Save backup, then Restore on the new phone. Tap Restore Purchases to turn Pro back on.

Rowbin Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription, made through the App Store. Use Settings → Restore Purchases if Pro doesn’t show on a device signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Voice counting (Pro). Say “next”, “back”, “undo” or “stop”. It uses your device’s on-device speech recognition only. If your device doesn’t support on-device recognition, voice counting stays off.

Widgets. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Rowbin.
