Rowbin privacy policy

Applies to the Rowbin app for Android, made by pocketappstudio. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

The short version

Rowbin collects no personal data. Everything you create stays on your phone.

What Rowbin stores

Your projects, counts, counters, notes, photos, patterns and pattern markup are saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage. Rowbin does not upload them to pocketappstudio or to anyone else.

What Rowbin does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Permissions and why

  • Vibrate: short vibrations for tap feedback. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional project timer notification. Rowbin asks first and works fully without it.
  • Microphone (optional, Rowbin Pro voice counting): only while you turn Voice on, Rowbin listens for a few command words ("next", "back", "undo", "stop") using your phone's own on-device speech recognition. Your voice is processed on your phone; Rowbin does not record, store or upload audio, and does not keep the recognised words. If your phone can't recognise speech on the device, voice counting stays off: Rowbin never falls back to online speech recognition. You can turn the microphone permission off at any time in Android settings.
  • Photos and patterns: Rowbin only reads a photo or PDF you pick yourself with Android's file or photo picker. It has no broad access to your files.
  • Internet and billing: Rowbin's own code sends nothing over the internet. The internet permission is there because Google Play Billing, a part of Google Play, uses it to confirm your Pro purchase.

Purchases

Rowbin Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles payment and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. Rowbin only learns whether you own Pro, and never sees your payment details.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you choose to save one, and go wherever you save them. Rowbin cannot see them. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Rowbin, so keep your own backups.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete everything Rowbin saved. Rowbin keeps no copy anywhere else, so deleted data cannot be recovered.

Children

Rowbin is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Rowbin is made by pocketappstudio. For questions, use the contact details on Rowbin's Google Play listing.
