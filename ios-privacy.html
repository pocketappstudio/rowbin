Rowbin privacy policy

Applies to the Rowbin app for iPhone and iPad, made by pocketappstudio. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

The short version

Rowbin collects no personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

What Rowbin stores

Your projects, counts, counters, notes, photos, patterns and pattern markup are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Rowbin does not upload them to pocketappstudio or to anyone else.

What Rowbin does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Permissions and why

Vibration (haptics): short taps from the Taptic Engine for tap feedback. You can turn this off in Settings → Vibrate on tap.

Microphone and Speech Recognition (optional, Rowbin Pro voice counting): only while you turn Voice on, Rowbin listens for a few command words ("next", "back", "undo", "stop") using your iPhone's own on-device speech recognition. Rowbin requires on-device recognition for every request, so your voice is processed on your device and is not sent to Apple or anyone else. Rowbin does not record, store or upload audio, does not keep the recognised words, and never reads anything aloud. If your device can't recognise speech on the device, voice counting stays off: Rowbin never falls back to online speech recognition. Microphone and speech data is not collected. You can turn either permission off at any time in iOS Settings → Rowbin.

Notifications: Rowbin for iPhone does not send notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

Photos and patterns: Rowbin only reads a photo or PDF you pick yourself with the system picker. It has no broad access to your photo library or files.

Widgets: your counts are shared with Rowbin's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets through storage on your device that only Rowbin can read.

Internet: Rowbin's own code sends nothing over the internet. Apple's App Store checks your Pro purchase.

Purchases

Rowbin Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple handles payment and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. Rowbin only learns whether you own Pro, and never sees your payment details.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you choose to save one, and go wherever you save them. Rowbin cannot see them. Keep your own backups.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use Settings → Erase everything in Rowbin, to delete everything Rowbin saved. Rowbin keeps no copy anywhere else, so deleted data cannot be recovered.

Children

Rowbin is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Rowbin is made by pocketappstudio. For questions, use the contact details on Rowbin's App Store listing, or pocketappstudio.gumroad.com.
