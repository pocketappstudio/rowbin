Rowbin: Free Row Counter for Knitting & Crochet

Rowbin is a free row counter for knitting and crochet that runs in your phone's browser. No ads, no account, no subscription. It saves your count on every tap, works offline, keeps your screen awake, and has undo, a repeat counter, notes and backup export. Two projects are free forever. Rowbin Pro is $14.99, a one-time purchase, no subscription, and adds a PDF pattern viewer with a line that follows your row, unlimited projects, a timer and dark mode.
